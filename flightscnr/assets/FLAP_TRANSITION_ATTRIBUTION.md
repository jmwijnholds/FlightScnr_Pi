# Split-flap transition sound

`flap_transition.mp3` is a recording of a real split-flap board from
[FlipOff](https://github.com/magnum6actual/flipoff) (magnum6actual).

`flap_click.wav` is one flap (~73 ms) sliced from that clip and mixed once
per turning tile, on the same stagger as the on-screen board.

- Source: https://github.com/magnum6actual/flipoff
- File: `js/flapAudio.js` (base64-decoded MPEG layer III)
